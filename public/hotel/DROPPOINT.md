## Workflow

1. Save photos with the exact filenames above (case-sensitive).
2. Aim for JPG, quality 80-85%, max 500KB each if possible.
3. Tell Claude: **"wired the photos"** — the data layer gets repointed and the site rebuilds.

Missing slots? No problem — those slots keep their current Unsplash stock images until you replace them. You can do this in batches.
